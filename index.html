XChange

讓新世代人才互通有無A talent accelerator for Taiwan’s internet industry

2015 年成立的數位教育 NGO，串連網路產業的新世代人才，共享產業知識與人脈。
與新北市政府合辦「互聯網大學」，每年公益培育 100 名大學生；
旗下天使創投已投資 11 家台灣科技新創。

Founded in 2015, XChange is a digital-education NGO that connects a new generation of talent in the internet industry to share knowledge and networks. Its “Internet University”, run with the New Taipei City Government, trains 100 university students a year at no cost, and its angel fund has invested in 11 Taiwanese tech startups.
